**The Federal Docket — September 29, 2026**


In a 5-4 opinion, the Supreme Court held in *Chatrie v. United States* that law enforcement obtaining Google "Location History" data amounts to a "search" under the Fourth Amendment, thus requiring a warrant.

 

Since the ruling, at least two federal courts have applied its reasoning to other cell phone technologies: a district court in Mississippi held that a warrant for a "tower dump" was an unconstitutional "general warrant," and the Fourth Circuit held that use of cell-site simulators constitutes a Fourth Amendment search.

### CLUSTER 3 — National Debt & Fiscal Policy

**Washington Times — October 8, 2026** *(Published within last 24 hours)*


The federal budget deficit ballooned to $2 trillion in the fiscal year that ended September 30, compared to $1.8 trillion last year — a 12% increase, according to a new CBO report.

 

CBO estimated that spending rose faster than revenues — $386 billion compared to $169 billion respectively — with government spending rising 6% against revenue growth of only 3%.
