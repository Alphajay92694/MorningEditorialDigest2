## CLUSTER 3 — National Debt & Fiscal Policy

**Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget / July 30, 2026 — New Social Security Benefit Taxation Proposals Released** [PIPELINE HIT]


CRFB released a new analysis today finding that while no single reform to Social Security benefit taxation would restore program solvency alone, expanded benefit taxation options could be part of a broader solvency package, with revenues allocated to whichever trust fund is most in need.



**CBO / February 2026 — Budget and Economic Outlook 2026–2036**


CBO projects the federal deficit will total $1.9 trillion in fiscal year 2026 and grow to $3.1 trillion by 2036; relative to the economy, the deficit stands at 5.8% of GDP this year and increases to 6.7% by 2036.

 

Debt held by the public is projected to rise from 101% of GDP in 2026 to 120% by 2036, well above the post-WWII record of 106%.

 [PIPELINE HIT]

**House Budget Committee / March 3, 2026 — Chairman Arrington Statement on CBO Long-Term Outlook**


Mandatory spending is projected to grow from 75% of the federal budget today to 83% by 2056, while interest spending on the national debt is projected to grow from 3.3% of GDP in 2026 to 6.9% by 2056.

 

Interest payments on the national debt will exceed all defense spending and surpass total discretionary spending by 2038.



**American Action Forum / June 9, 2026 — 2026 Social Security & Medicare Trustees Report Highlights**


The Social Security Trustees estimate the OASI trust fund will exhaust its reserves by 2032, one year earlier than last year's projection.

 

The report shows the OASI program "remains unsustainable and will be unable to meet the needs of current and future beneficiaries absent significant reforms."
