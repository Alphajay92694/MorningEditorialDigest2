## CLUSTER 1 — Constitutional & Law Enforcement

**SCOTUSblog, June 30, 2026**


The Supreme Court held in *United States v. Chatrie* that police officers conducted a Fourth Amendment search when they acquired a suspect's location data from Google via geofence warrant, establishing that individuals retain a reasonable expectation of privacy in cellphone location information; Justice Kagan authored the majority, with Alito and Barrett in dissent.



**Congress.gov / CRS, February 12, 2026**


On January 14, 2026, the Supreme Court in *Case v. Montana* resolved the Fourth Amendment standard for warrantless home entry during public safety emergencies, unanimously holding that law enforcement need not possess probable cause — reasoning that probable cause governs criminal investigative functions, not public safety responsibilities.



**Law Cornell / Review Journal (standing background), 2024**
[PIPELINE HIT] 

The Supreme Court's 6-3 decision in *Culley v. Marshall*, authored by Justice Kavanaugh, ruled that due process requires only a timely forfeiture hearing and not a separate post-seizure preliminary hearing for property owners in civil forfeiture cases

 — a ruling that, 

while arising under Alabama law, has direct consequences for the forfeiture laws of many states, as well as federal civil forfeiture proceedings.

## CLUSTER 2 — Military & National Security

**Stars and Stripes / NGAUS, April 21, 2026**


The Pentagon formally unveiled its $1.5 trillion budget for fiscal 2027, calling for raises of 5% to 7% for troops, an increase of 44,000 personnel in the overall force, and $750 billion on defense capabilities and weapons systems; Space Force Lt. Gen. Steven P. Whitney noted the size avoids the traditional "trade between investment in the capabilities we need and the readiness of our force."



**House Appropriations Committee (Minority), late June 2026**


The House Appropriations Committee advanced a 2027 defense funding bill setting the Pentagon budget at more than $1 trillion — the largest in U.S. history and a $234 billion increase over 2026.



**Military.com / SOFX, June 25–26, 2026**
[PIPELINE HIT] 

A SOCOM study found that special operations forces (SOF) personnel face an 18% higher cancer risk than conventional service members, with elevated rates of melanoma and testicular cancer; the study analyzed health data from 231,733 SOF personnel and more than 5.1 million non-SOF service members who entered service between 2001 and 2023.



**The Verardo Group / Federal News Network, July 2, 2026**


The VA's Undersecretary for Health John Bartrum resigned effective July 6 with no public explanation, having overseen the VA's healthcare system serving more than 9 million veterans; separately, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth signed a memorandum on June 29, 2026, establishing a new senior Pentagon role to oversee autonomous weapons systems development across all military branches.



**Military.com, April–May 2026**


The newly introduced Take Care of America's Veterans Act combines more than 60 veterans bills into a single $57 billion package, including the Major Richard Star Act, the Love Lives On Act, expanded benefits for catastrophically disabled veterans, and VA modernization initiatives

 — though 

the central dispute remains who pays: veterans have already paid through their service and health, and if the nation wants to expand benefits, the question of offsets is unresolved.
