### CLUSTER 3 — National Debt & Fiscal Policy

**Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget / June 9, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


The 2026 Trustees Report projects Social Security's OASI trust fund will be insolvent in 2032 and Medicare's Hospital Insurance trust fund will deplete its reserves by 2033.

 

Social Security's 75-year solvency gap grew 16% relative to last year, with over half of the deterioration due to lower projected fertility rates, a third from lower immigration assumptions, and a quarter attributable to the One Big Beautiful Bill Act's reduction of revenue from income taxation of Social Security benefits.



**CBO / Congressional Budget Office (2026 Outlook)**


CBO projects the federal budget deficit in FY2026 at $1.9 trillion, with federal debt rising to 120 percent of GDP in 2036.

 

The 2025 reconciliation act and administrative actions related to immigration increased CBO's deficit projections by $4.7 trillion and $0.5 trillion, respectively, after macroeconomic effects — partially offset by higher tariffs reducing deficits by $3 trillion.



**House Budget Committee / March 3, 2026**


Interest spending on the national debt is projected to grow from 3.3% of GDP in 2026 to 6.9% by 2056, and interest payments will surpass all discretionary spending by 2038.

 Chairman Arrington called the structural imbalance driven by autopilot entitlement spending "the single greatest danger to our nation's prosperity."
