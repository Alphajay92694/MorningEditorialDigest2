## CLUSTER 3 — National Debt & Fiscal Policy

**The Hill — August 11, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


CBO revised the FY2026 federal deficit projection upward by $200 billion to $2.1 trillion; higher spending on Social Security, Medicare, and Medicaid accounted for roughly 86% of the yearly rise in outlays.

 

The upward revision is "mostly" driven by U.S. Customs and Border Protection collecting a "smaller-than-expected" amount in tariff duties, after the Supreme Court struck down levies Trump imposed under IEEPA.



**CRFB / Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget — June 9, 2026**


The 2026 Trustees Reports project Social Security's OASI trust fund will be insolvent in 2032, while Medicare's Hospital Insurance trust fund will deplete its reserves by 2033.

 

The looming insolvency of Social Security's retirement program will lead to a 22% benefit cut when today's youngest retirees turn 68, growing to 38% by 2100.



**Cato Institute / Bipartisan Policy Center — February–June 2026**

Under CBO's baseline, the average interest rate on all federal debt will eventually exceed nominal economic growth (R>G), which could represent the start of a debt spiral.
