## CLUSTER 2 — Military & National Security

**Breaking Defense / July 9, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]

The Army will shift $1.3 billion away from National Guard training and admin accounts, while Army missile procurement accounts take a $235 million hit.



**Federal News Network / July 15, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT — PUBLISHED TODAY]

## CLUSTER 3 — National Debt & Fiscal Policy

**CBO / February 2026 (latest baseline)** [PIPELINE HIT]


CBO projects the federal budget deficit in fiscal year 2026 at $1.9 trillion, with federal debt rising to 120 percent of GDP by 2036.

 

Autopilot mandatory spending — interest costs and programs like Social Security and Medicare — is almost entirely responsible for this growth; by 2036, those four line items will account for 73 percent of total federal spending, consuming nearly 100 percent of all federal revenue.



**Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget / June 9, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


The 2026 Trustees project that Social Security's OASI trust fund will be insolvent in 2032, or 2034 if funds are reallocated from the disability trust fund, while Medicare's Hospital Insurance trust fund will deplete its reserves by 2033.

 

The looming insolvency of Social Security's retirement program will lead to a 22% benefit cut when today's youngest retirees turn 68.



**Cato Institute / February 2026**


Net interest — the cost of servicing the national debt — will double from $970 billion in 2025 to $2.1 trillion in 2036; by 2036, interest payments will nearly equal all discretionary spending combined.

 

CBO estimates the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA) will increase deficits by $4.7 trillion over 10 years.



**Bleeding Heartland / July 12, 2026**


The HI fund has a functioning trust fund, and the Trustees' Report projects it will run out in early 2033, with an 11 percent shortfall at that point.
