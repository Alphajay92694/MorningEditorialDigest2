### CLUSTER 1 — Constitutional & Law Enforcement

**Pipe & Gas Journal / July 10, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


The Supreme Court has agreed to hear *Hoffmann v. WBI Energy Transmission Inc.*, granted certiorari on June 29, which will determine whether Natural Gas Act certificate holders exercising federal eminent domain authority must pay attorney's fees when state law permits such recovery — marking the Court's second interpretation of Section 7 of the NGA in five years.



**Rhode Island Current / July 28, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


A federal judge voided the Town of Johnston's eminent domain seizure of 31 acres of private land, ruling that "when a municipality elects to invoke the power of eminent domain, it must proceed in the manner prescribed by law," finding the town's reliance on a bare charter provision constitutionally insufficient.



**RFD-TV / July 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


A commentary piece on the pending SCOTUS NGA case warns that reading implied federal preemption into the Natural Gas Act risks "upsetting the constitutional allocation of authority" between federal and state governments while diminishing property protections long afforded to farmers, ranchers, and rural landowners.



**Davis Wright Tremaine / February 13, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


The Supreme Court is set to hear consolidated FCC civil forfeiture cases — *Verizon v. FCC* and *FCC v. AT&T* — in which its ruling will determine whether there are constitutional limits on administrative enforcement power and the imposition of monetary penalties by federal agencies.

### CLUSTER 3 — National Debt & Fiscal Policy

**Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget / July 30, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


A new CRFB white paper released two days ago finds that while no reform to Social Security benefit taxation alone would be sufficient to restore Social Security or Medicare solvency, aggressive restructuring of benefit taxation could close 90% of Social Security's solvency gap and delay insolvency to around 2090.



**SSA Trustees Report / June 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


The 2026 Social Security Trustees Report projects the OASI trust fund will pay 100% of scheduled benefits only until the fourth quarter of 2032 — one quarter earlier than last year's projection — after which continuing income will cover only 78% of total scheduled benefits.



**CRFB / June 9, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


The 2026 Trustees Reports project OASI insolvency in 2032 and Medicare Hospital Insurance trust fund depletion by 2033, while Social Security's 75-year actuarial shortfall has grown 16% year-over-year and Medicare HI's shortfall has grown 33%.



**The Hill / CBO / February 2026**


CBO projects the federal deficit and national debt will grow by trillions over the next decade, with CBO Director Phillip Swagel stating, "Our budget projections continue to indicate that the fiscal trajectory is not sustainable."

 

The changes are driven primarily by the One Big Beautiful Bill, higher tariffs, and lower immigration — with the reconciliation law alone anticipated to increase the deficit by $4.7 trillion between 2026 and 2035.
