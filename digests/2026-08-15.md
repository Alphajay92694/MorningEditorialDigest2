In CBO's projections, the federal budget deficit in fiscal year 2026 is $1.9 trillion, and federal debt rises to 120 percent of GDP in 2036.

 

That trend is driven by greater spending on Social Security and Medicare and growth in net interest costs, only partly offset by declining outlays for discretionary programs.
