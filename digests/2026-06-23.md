### CLUSTER 1 — Constitutional & Law Enforcement

**Supreme Court of the United States / June 23, 2026**


Today the Court ruled in *Cisco Systems, Inc. v. Doe* (24-856) that courts may not create new rights of action to remedy violations of international law under the Alien Tort Statute, and that neither the ATS nor the Torture Victim Protection Act imposes liability for aiding-and-abetting offenses.

 Significant narrowing of third-party corporate liability exposure; secondary implications for federal common law expansion.

**Supreme Court of the United States / June 23, 2026**


The Court also ruled today in *Exxon Mobil Corp. v. Corporación Cimex, S.A.* (24-699), holding that the Helms-Burton Act abrogates sovereign immunity of Cuban agencies and instrumentalities, and that plaintiffs need not satisfy FSIA's enumerated exceptions to sue.

 Watch for Commerce Clause and separation-of-powers commentary as this ruling lands.

**SCOTUSblog / June 22, 2026**


With 12 decisions still pending this term, the Court has scheduled additional opinion announcements for Thursday, June 25.

 High-value opinions remain outstanding; monitor the docket through end of week.

**SCOTUSblog / April 27, 2026**
[PIPELINE HIT] 

In oral argument in *Chatrie v. United States*, Justice Gorsuch raised the concern that a ruling permitting warrantless access to geofence location data would equally expose all cloud-stored data—emails, photos, documents—to government search without a warrant.

 A ruling is expected imminently; Fourth Amendment digital-surveillance angle is op-ed-ready.

### CLUSTER 2 — Military & National Security

The White House released its FY2027 budget blueprint with a record 44% increase in defense spending; the $1.5 trillion request builds on the record $1 trillion for FY2026 and includes tiered pay boosts for all military grades.

### CLUSTER 3 — National Debt & Fiscal Policy

**Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget / ~June 9, 2026**
[PIPELINE HIT] 

CRFB warns that in just six years the Social Security retirement fund will exhaust reserves, with Medicare running out half a year later; today's youngest retirees will be turning 68 and 69 respectively when the cuts hit, and "our leaders have no plan" to prevent an abrupt 22% Social Security benefit cut or 11% Medicare payment cut.



**American Action Forum / ~June 9, 2026**


The 2026 Social Security Trustees report projects the OASI trust fund will exhaust reserves by 2032—one year earlier than last year's projection—while the theoretically combined OASDI trust funds will be insolvent by 2034.



**Fortune / June 11, 2026**


The CRFB notes that Social Security's projected shortfall is a full 16% worse than last year's, attributable largely to the tax cuts in the "One Big Beautiful Bill" and worsening demographics, while Medicare's shortfall is 33% worse.



**Congressional Budget Office / February 11, 2026**


CBO projects the FY2026 federal budget deficit at $1.9 trillion; federal debt held by the public rises from 101% of GDP this year to 120% in 2036, surpassing its previous historical high of 106% set in 1946.



**Bipartisan Policy Center / ~June 9, 2026**


A CRFB analysis of CBO's February 2026 baseline shows the average interest rate paid on the national debt could exceed the economic growth rate starting in FY2031, at which point the U.S. risks a self-reinforcing debt spiral absent significant fiscal reforms.
