### CLUSTER 3 — National Debt & Fiscal Policy

**SSA / June 9, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


The 2026 Trustees Reports project Social Security's OASI trust fund will be insolvent in 2032 (or 2034 if funds are reallocated from SSDI), while Medicare's Hospital Insurance trust fund will deplete reserves by 2033; Social Security's 75-year actuarial shortfall widened 16% from last year's projection to 4.42% of taxable payroll.



**Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget / June 9, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]

**Bipartisan Policy Center / ongoing 2026**


A CRFB analysis of CBO's February 2026 baseline projects that the average interest rate paid on the national debt could exceed the economic growth rate starting in FY2031—creating conditions for a potential debt spiral in which rising interest costs suppress growth and further compound debt.



**CBO / February 2026**


CBO projects the federal deficit will reach $1.9 trillion in FY2026, with federal debt rising to 120% of GDP by 2036, surpassing the previous record of 106% set in 1946.

**CalPERS PERSpective / March 20, 2026**
