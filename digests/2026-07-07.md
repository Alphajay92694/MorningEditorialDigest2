**Virginia Lawyers Weekly / July 6, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


The U.S. Supreme Court held in a 6-3 decision on June 29 that police investigating the robbery of a Chesterfield County credit union conducted a Fourth Amendment search when they acquired a suspect's cellphone location data from Google via a geofence warrant.

 

Writing for the majority, Justice Kagan sent the case back to a lower court to determine whether the search was "reasonable" under the Fourth Amendment.
