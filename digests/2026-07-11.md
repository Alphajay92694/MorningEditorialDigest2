### CLUSTER 3 — National Debt & Fiscal Policy

**Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget | July 9, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


The U.S. borrowed $1.4 trillion in the first nine months of FY2026 — $35 billion more than the same period in FY2025 — with CRFB President Maya MacGuineas warning that new tax cuts and spending increases have now pushed the FY2026 deficit above last year's level and projecting total borrowing of $2 trillion or more this fiscal year.



**The Hill / CBO | February 2026**


CBO Director Phillip Swagel stated plainly: "Our budget projections continue to indicate that the fiscal trajectory is not sustainable."

 

CBO attributed the deterioration in large part to three factors: the One Big Beautiful Bill Act signed into law by President Trump, higher tariffs, and lower immigration rates.



**American Action Forum | June 9, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


The 2026 Social Security Trustees Report shows the OASI program remains unsustainable, with the trust fund now projected to exhaust reserves by 2032 — one year earlier than the prior year's projection.

 

On its present course, the program is on track to reduce benefits by 17 percent for all beneficiaries in just eight years absent reform.



**CRFB | June 2026**


Social Security's 75-year solvency gap has grown 16% relative to last year's projections; over half of this deterioration stems from lower projected fertility rates, with a quarter attributable directly to the One Big Beautiful Bill Act's reduction in revenue from income taxation of Social Security benefits.
