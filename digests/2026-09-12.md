### CLUSTER 1 — Constitutional & Law Enforcement

**Wisner Baum / September 11, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


In one of the most consequential preemption decisions in pesticide litigation in decades, the Supreme Court ruled 7–2 in *Monsanto Co. v. Durnell* that federal pesticide law preempts state-law "failure to warn" claims related to product labels.

 

The majority indicated its reasoning extends beyond pesticides to other federally regulated industries

 — a significant Commerce Clause and Supremacy Clause victory with broad implications for federal regulatory primacy over state tort law.

**The Regulatory Review / September 1, 2026**


The *Durnell* preemption holding is now being cited alongside *Trump v. Slaughter* — in which the Court overruled *Humphrey's Executor* — as evidence of an accelerating term-over-term consolidation of federal authority

, prompting renewed academic debate over the reach of regulatory preemption doctrine.

**MMM-Online / June 26, 2026** *(Background context, active in litigation pipeline)*


The Court overturned a $1.25 million verdict awarded to John Durnell, a Missouri man who alleged non-Hodgkin lymphoma from Roundup use, holding that states cannot require a cancer warning when the EPA has approved the label without one.
