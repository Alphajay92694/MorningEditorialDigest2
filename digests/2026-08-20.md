## CLUSTER 3 — National Debt & Fiscal Policy

**CBO / Congressional Budget Office — February 2026 (primary baseline)** [PIPELINE HIT]


The federal deficit totals $1.9 trillion in FY2026 and is projected to grow to $3.1 trillion by 2036; relative to GDP, the deficit stands at 5.8% in 2026 and rises to 6.7% by 2036, well above the 50-year historical average of 3.8%.

 

Debt held by the public rises from 101% of GDP in 2026 to 120% in 2036, surpassing the prior record of 106% set just after World War II.

 

The upward trend is driven by greater spending on Social Security and Medicare and growth in net interest costs, only partly offset by declining discretionary outlays.



**House Budget Committee (Chairman Arrington) — March 3, 2026**
