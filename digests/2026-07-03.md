**Penn Carey Law / May 2024 [SCOTUS Precedent Active in Litigation]** [PIPELINE HIT]


In *Culley v. Marshall*, a 6-3 decision authored by Justice Kavanaugh held that a property owner is entitled to a timely forfeiture hearing, but not to a separate post-seizure hearing to contest retention of property while awaiting final forfeiture adjudication.

 

The ruling leaves intact a system under which agencies seize property from owners never charged or convicted of a crime, while imposing low burdens of proof on government and requiring owners to affirmatively prove innocence.
