Civil forfeiture laws proliferated in the 1980s to target drug kingpins but have since evolved into a revenue mechanism for police agencies, which are often allowed to keep a portion of seizure proceeds.



**Penn Carey Law / Money Laundering Watch (May 2024):** [PIPELINE HIT]


The Supreme Court's *Culley v. Marshall* decision held that a separate preliminary hearing is not constitutionally required when police seize property in civil forfeiture; in a 6-3 ruling authored by Justice Kavanaugh, the Court held that a property owner is entitled to a timely forfeiture hearing but not a separate post-seizure hearing to recover property while awaiting the outcome.

 

Dissenting and concurring opinions signal that the legal and social debates over civil forfeiture practices are far from over.

## CLUSTER 3 — National Debt & Fiscal Policy

**Congressional Budget Office / CBO.gov (ongoing baseline):**


The federal deficit totals $1.9 trillion in fiscal year 2026 and is projected to grow to $3.1 trillion by 2036; relative to the economy, the deficit is 5.8% of GDP in 2026, rising to 6.7% by 2036, well above the 50-year historical average of 3.8%.



**CBO Director's Statement (early 2026):** [PIPELINE HIT]


The balance of Social Security's Old-Age and Survivors Insurance Trust Fund is now projected to be exhausted in 2032, one year earlier than previously projected.

 

Net outlays for interest on the national debt are projected to go from $1.0 trillion in 2026 to $2.1 trillion in 2036, rising from 3.3% to 4.6% of GDP.



**Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget (June 9, 2026):** [PIPELINE HIT]


The Social Security Trustees warn that Social Security faces the largest financial imbalance since 1977 and is closer to insolvency than any time since 1983; in just six years, nearly all retirees and survivors on Social Security will face a deep 22% benefit cut unless reforms are enacted.

 

A quarter of the deterioration in the solvency gap is attributable to the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, which reduced revenue from the income taxation of Social Security benefits.



**House Budget Committee — Chairman Arrington (March 3, 2026):**


Interest spending on the national debt is projected to grow from 3.3% of GDP in 2026 to 6.9% by 2056, and interest payments will surpass total discretionary spending by 2038.
