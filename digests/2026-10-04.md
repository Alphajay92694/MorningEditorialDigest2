### CLUSTER 1 — Constitutional & Law Enforcement

**SCOTUSblog / Rocky Mountain Voice — October 3–5, 2026** [PIPELINE HIT]


On the first day of the October 2026 Term, the Court will hear *Suncor Energy, Inc. v. County Commissioners of Boulder County*, posing the issue of whether suits under state law against energy companies for their role in causing climate change are preempted by federal law.

 

It is the first time the Court will decide on the merits whether federal law bars state-law climate-damages claims of this kind against fossil-fuel companies.

 

The Trump administration argues that federal law prevents Boulder from using state tort law to impose liability for the consequences of global greenhouse-gas emissions.

 This is the preemption and Commerce Clause case to track for the term; a ruling for the energy companies would significantly curtail state-level regulatory overreach via the tort system.

**Reason.com — September 28, 2026**


Justice Alito has recused from *Suncor v. Boulder*, which is almost certainly the most significant case of the Supreme Court's October sitting and may be among the most consequential of the entire OT 2026 term.

 The recusal narrows the conservative majority's margin on what could be a landmark preemption ruling.

**Third Circuit / JDSupra — 2026**


In *United States v. Mendoza*, the Third Circuit held that a hotel guest does not retain a reasonable expectation of privacy in a hotel room five hours after checkout time, affirming denial of a motion to suppress.

 A modest but notable narrowing of Fourth Amendment protections in transient-lodging contexts.
